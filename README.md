Entry for the [Sorry, I Heard That Wrong](https://make.fun/c/u-kiencx-sorry-i-heard-that-wrong-1858c729) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` writes a single line — the apology a smart speaker gives its owner after mishearing them — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Sorry, I heard 'remind me to water the plants' as 'remind me to water the planets.' Mars is now damp. I've apologised to NASA.

Run it locally with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=<dir> python3 main.py`; the output is one line of 126 characters, inside the 140-character limit.
